Sobel-only ablation — structural cue without depth
Result: 2/4 flights successful

With Sobel as the only virtual sensor, the drone correctly detects the
horizontal step edges and receives a clear per-step "up" signal, but the
absence of a depth reference leaves it "blind" at the end of the ramp.

Flight 1 (f1): Successful.
Flight 2 (f2): The drone kept climbing after the stairs had ended and hit the wall.
Flight 3 (f3): Landed prematurely, before the end of the ramp.
Flight 4 (f4): Successful.

Takeaway: Sobel supplies the per-step "up" cue but, lacking a depth reference,
cannot tell when the ramp ends — so it either overshoots the landing or lands early.
